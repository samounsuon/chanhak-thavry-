Hello! I'm Thavry Chanhak

I'm Thavry Chanhak, a passionate UI/UX and Graphic Designer born on September 28, 2006, in Prey Veng, Cambodia. I specialize in creating user-friendly digital experiences with clean, intuitive interfaces and strong visual design.

I recently completed my Associate Degree in Information Technology at Passerelles Numériques Cambodia (PNC). During my studies, I gained hands-on experience in UI/UX design, including user research, wireframing, and prototyping, along with web design and basic cloud deployment.

After graduating from PNC, I worked as a Graphic Designer at TH F&B Co., Ltd., where I focused on creating visual content and strengthening my skills in branding and digital design.

I'm passionate about both UI/UX and graphic design, and I enjoy combining creativity with usability to build meaningful digital experiences.
